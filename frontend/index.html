THE PROBLEM

Information Silos & Unanticipated Subsurface Hazards

Real-time telemetry from the active rig provides live parameters (WOB, ROP, Torque, SPP), but critical institutional memory remains trapped in static PDF Well Completion Reports, Daily Drilling Reports, and offset logs. When the bit enters a hazardous zone, engineers lack instant, contextual insight into what offset wells experienced at that exact depth.

Core Capabilities

Offset Well Intelligence

Evaluates offset wells across 7 technical comparability dimensions (distance, formation, depth, trajectory, parameters, reservoir, events).

Look-Ahead Hazard Detection

Scans +50m, +100m, and +150m ahead of the active bit to proactively project historical loss zones, kicks, and pack-offs before penetration.

Evidence-Grounded Assistant

Responses are grounded in authorized PetroNexus records and retrieved evidence. When sufficient evidence is unavailable, PetroNexus reports that limitation.
